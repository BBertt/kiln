Before you start

Every measurement below is wrong until #609 lands. The harness swallows the invalid tool calls production records, so its tool-call numbers understate the cost. Confirm BytePlus's model IDs and rate limits in their console before sizing a sweep.

Do not spend it on

  • Volume code or docs. Review cost eats the gain.
  • A new abstraction, a fine-tune, or anything needing a human per item.
  • Looping the same six fixtures. Widen the corpus instead.
