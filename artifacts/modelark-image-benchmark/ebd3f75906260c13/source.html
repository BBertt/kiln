    The 70 matched images

    Every model saw the same seven prompts at 4:3, twice. Filter the set, compare one prompt across models, or open any preview for its measured metadata.

    Preview resolution

    Every tile is a sanitised 400 px WebP preview generated from the measured output. Full 2K images remain local and are not published. These previews are for composition and text inspection, not a full-resolution deliverable.

    All eight Arcane ratios

    Three finalists, one coastal-pavilion prompt, every app ratio including the 9:16 that the old spike missed. Requested and returned dimensions matched exactly. Named-ratio error is separate: two 4.5 presets are ~0.125% off 16:9 and 9:16; every other cell is exact.

    4:5 has no official 2K row; the explicit 1728×2160 size was accepted exactly by every finalist and is exactly 0.8. For 4.5, the official 16:9 preset is 2848×1600 (ratio 1.78, about 0.125% above 16/9) and 9:16 is 1600×2848 (about 0.125% below 9/16). All requested pixel sizes were honored; only the named-ratio semantics differ.
